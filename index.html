About

Cloud infra now, AI/ML infra next

I'm a Tech Lead based in Warsaw, working as a contractor with Novo Nordisk. I lead a two-person cloud team building multi-cloud VM provisioning and golden-image platforms on AWS and Azure. My focus is treating infrastructure as something you draft, review, and version — not something you click together by hand.

I'm extending that same discipline into AI/ML infrastructure: applying infra-as-code rigor to the systems that run models, not just the systems that run VMs. That's the throughline behind the Forward Deployed Engineer direction — not a career pivot, an extension of the same practice.
